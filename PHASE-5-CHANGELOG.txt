LEX MARKET — PHASE 5
Personalization, Notifications & App Experience

WHAT'S NEW
- Personalized "Picked for you" home section based on browsing/category activity.
- Local shopping-interest signals for lightweight recommendations.
- Wishlist alerts for low-stock items and detected price drops.
- New Alerts panel in the header.
- Improved cart/wishlist badges across desktop and mobile navigation.
- Account shopping-activity panel with recent views, saved items and interests.
- PWA manifest for app-like installation on supported browsers.
- Service worker with network-first caching and offline fallback.
- Existing Phase 1–4 Firebase, seller, admin, checkout, cart, wishlist and compare functionality preserved.

NOTES
- Personalization is stored locally in the browser; no new customer profile fields are required.
- Price alerts compare a browser's saved catalog price snapshot against later catalog prices.
- PWA installation is browser/device dependent and normally works when served over HTTPS (for example GitHub Pages).
